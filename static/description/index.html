Why Master Commission?

Calculating commissions based on profit margins rather than total revenue is a nightmare for most businesses. Partner splits, dynamic product margins, and monthly liquidations often require endless spreadsheets. Master Commission integrates seamlessly into your Sales workflow to automate everything.

Partner Management

Register sales partners and define custom split percentages. Track all partners in a unified dashboard.

Automated Margins

Commissions are calculated based on the actual profit margin of products, ensuring your company always stays profitable.

Monthly Liquidations

Consolidate all generated commissions at the end of the month and liquidate them automatically into accounting bills.

A Powerful Interface

Comprehensive Dashboard

Get a bird's-eye view of your business performance. The dashboard displays the total generated profit, pending commissions, and historical charts so you can track growth effortlessly.

Register Your Partners

Easily manage all your business partners. Assign them to sales orders and define how the profit is split according to your commercial agreements.

Consolidate Sales Data

When an order is confirmed, the system immediately calculates the utility and logs the corresponding commission records for each partner. Complete traceability for every cent.

Monthly Liquidations

At the end of the month, execute bulk liquidations. The module will grab all pending commissions and automatically draft vendor bills in the accounting app so you can pay your partners.

Transparency for Partners

Partners can log in and view their own "My Commissions" panel to see exactly what they've earned, what is pending, and what has been paid. Say goodbye to disputes.

Flexible Configuration

Adapt the module to your specific business needs from the General Settings. Define accounts, journals, and liquidation behaviors easily.
